# Centennial College: Program Offerings and Credentials

Centennial College offers over 160 full-time programs, along with part-time and continuing education options, across five campuses and eight academic Schools.

## Types of Credentials Offered

### Certificate
- Typically one year or less of full-time study.
- Designed to give students job-ready skills for a fast path into employment in a specific career.

### Diploma
- Typically two years of full-time study.
- Provides broader technical and theoretical training than a certificate, preparing students for direct entry into a career field.

### Advanced Diploma
- Typically three years of full-time study.
- Builds on diploma-level training with more advanced or specialized coursework; common in engineering technology and business fields.
- Fast-Track versions are available for students transferring credit from prior post-secondary study, allowing completion in a shorter time frame (e.g., a 3-year advanced diploma completed in 2 years).

### Bachelor's Degree (Honours)
- Four-year programs combining practical, career-focused skills with theoretical/academic depth.
- Examples include the Honours Bachelor of Business Administration – International Business, the Honours Bachelor of Information Technology (Computer and Communication Networks), and the Honours Bachelor of Public Relations Management.
- These programs are offered under time-limited ministerial consent (degree-granting authority is renewed periodically by the Ontario Ministry of Colleges and Universities).

### Graduate Certificate
- Requires previous post-secondary education (a university degree or three-year college diploma) to enter.
- Shorter, more cost-effective, and employment-focused compared to a university Master's or PhD.
- Popular with internationally educated students seeking Canadian credentials and work experience.
- Over 40 Graduate Certificate programs are offered across business, health sciences, engineering technology, and communications/media.

### Other Program Types
- **Apprenticeship programs:** combine in-class study with on-the-job training over two to five years.
- **Foundations / College and University Preparation programs:** help students build academic and English-language skills before entering a diploma or degree program.
- **Micro-credentials:** short, skills-focused, stackable credentials for targeted upskilling.

## General Admission Requirements

### Domestic Students — Certificate, Diploma, or Advanced Diploma programs
- Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD) or equivalent.
- Applicants without an OSSD who are 19 years of age or older may apply as mature students.
- Specific programs may require minimum grades in courses like English or Math, and may require additional materials such as portfolios, auditions, writing samples, or interviews.

### Domestic Students — Bachelor's Degree programs
- Generally require six Grade 12 University (U) or University/College (M) preparation courses with a minimum overall average (commonly around 65% or higher), including specific required courses (e.g., Grade 12 Advanced Functions for technology-related degrees).

### Domestic Students — Fast-Track or Graduate Certificate programs
- A university degree or three-year college diploma is typically required.
- Some programs will consider applicants with a partial degree (minimum two years) or a two-year diploma plus at least two years of related work experience, at the College's discretion.

### International Students
- Must meet the same academic requirements as domestic students for their chosen credential level (equivalent secondary or post-secondary credentials, assessed for Canadian equivalency).
- Must demonstrate English language proficiency, since all coursework is delivered in English. Accepted tests include IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, Duolingo English Test, CAEL, and ITEP, or Centennial's own English admission testing.
- Typical minimum requirements are approximately IELTS 6.0–6.5 overall (with no band below 5.5–6.0) for most programs; some postgraduate programs require higher scores (e.g., 7.0 overall for certain business or IT security programs).
- Required documents generally include academic transcripts, proof of English proficiency, a valid passport, and — once accepted — a valid Canadian study permit.
- International applicants apply directly to Centennial (not through ontariocolleges.ca) and are supported by regional International Admissions teams.

## Flagship Programs Across Domains

### Software Engineering Technology – Artificial Intelligence
- **School:** Engineering Technology and Applied Science
- **Credential:** Advanced Diploma (optional co-op; online and on-campus versions available)
- Focuses on AI application development, machine learning, natural language processing, data visualization, big data fundamentals, image recognition, recommender systems, and digital ethics.
- Open to graduates of Software Engineering Technology or similar programs, and to holders of a degree/diploma in a computing-related field.
- Fast-Track options allow qualified applicants to enter directly into Semester 3.

### Honours Bachelor of Business Administration – International Business
- **School:** School of Business
- **Credential:** Four-year Honours Bachelor Degree (8 semesters)
- Prepares graduates for roles in international trade, logistics, and investment.

### Honours Bachelor of Public Relations Management
- **Schools:** Communications, Media, Arts and Design (with School of Business)
- **Credential:** Four-year Honours Bachelor Degree
- A first-of-its-kind Canadian program combining public relations training with core business coursework.

### Honours Bachelor of Information Technology (Computer and Communication Networks)
- **School:** Engineering Technology and Applied Science
- **Credential:** Four-year Honours Bachelor Degree (9 semesters)
- The first and longest-running four-year IT bachelor's degree offered by an Ontario college; focuses on networking, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and telecommunications.

### Culinary and Hospitality Programs
- **School:** Hospitality, Tourism and Culinary Arts (Progress Campus)
- Hands-on training in specialized culinary labs, supporting careers in culinary arts, hotel operations, and tourism management.

### Aviation and Aerospace Programs
- **School:** School of Transportation (Downsview Campus)
- Includes Aviation Technician and Aerospace Manufacturing training in industry-aligned labs and hangars.

## Quick Reference for the AI Receptionist
- **"What's the difference between a diploma and an advanced diploma?" →** 2 years vs. 3 years of full-time study, respectively.
- **"Can I go straight into a degree program from high school?" →** Yes, if OSSD/Grade 12 U/M course requirements are met.
- **"I already have a degree — what are my options?" →** Graduate Certificate or Fast-Track programs.
- **"What English test score do I need?" →** Varies by program; general guidance is IELTS 6.0–6.5 overall, but always confirm with the specific program page.
- **"Does Centennial offer [specific program]?" →** Check the Full-Time Program Directory above, organized by School. If not listed there, it may be a part-time, micro-credential, or continuing-education offering — direct the student to centennialcollege.ca/programs-and-courses to search the live catalogue.
