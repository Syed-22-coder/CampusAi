# Centennial College: Campuses and Facilities

Centennial College operates five main campuses across Toronto, Ontario, plus a dedicated Performing Arts Centre. All campuses offer general hours of operation Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 11 p.m., and Saturday to Sunday, 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. All campuses have 24-hour security.

## Progress Campus
**Address:** 941 Progress Ave., Scarborough, ON M1G 3T8

Progress is Centennial's largest campus and is home to the School of Business and the School of Hospitality, Tourism and Culinary Arts, along with Advanced Manufacturing and Automation Engineering Technology, and many Child Studies and Community Service programs.

**Facilities include:**
- Library and Learning Centre
- Athletics and Wellness Centre (gym, fitness facilities)
- CCSAI Student Centre (student association hub)
- The Local — a student-run restaurant
- On-site Bookstore
- Centennial Place Residence (student housing)
- Event Centre
- Multiple food service locations

## Morningside Campus
**Address:** 755 Morningside Ave., Toronto, ON M1C 5J9

Located in the Rouge Valley Conservation Area, Morningside is an environmentally friendly building home to the School of Community and Health Studies, plus selected Engineering Technology and Applied Science programs.

**Facilities include:**
- Library and Learning Centre
- Fitness Centre
- CCSAI student lounge
- Child Care Centre / Early Learning Centre
- Censation Esthetics Student Clinic
- Massage Therapy Program Public Clinic
- Food and entertainment options
- On-site Bookstore

## Ashtonbee Campus
**Address:** 75 Ashtonbee Rd., Toronto, ON M1L 4N4

Ashtonbee is Centennial's automotive-focused campus and one of the largest automotive training centres in Canada. It is also home to selected Community and Health Studies programs.

**Facilities include:**
- Library
- Athletic and Wellness Centre
- CCSAI student lounge
- On-site Bookstore
- Specialized automotive labs and training bays
- Nearby shopping and dining

## Story Arts Centre Campus
**Address:** 951 Carlaw Ave., Toronto, ON M4K 3M2

Story Arts Centre is Centennial's creative arts hub, home to communications, media, arts, and design programs, with industry-focused studios and labs. Note: programs at Story Arts Centre are scheduled to move to Progress Campus in Fall 2026.

**Facilities include:**
- Library and Learning Centre
- Student Lounge
- Fitness Centre
- Nearby shopping and dining along the Danforth
- Adjacent Performing Arts Centre (2200 Eglinton Ave. E), which houses performing arts programs

## Downsview Campus
**Address:** 65 Carl Hall Rd., Toronto, ON M3K 2C1

Downsview is Centennial's aerospace-focused campus, supporting Aviation Technician and Aerospace Manufacturing programs through hands-on, industry-aligned training.

**Facilities include:**
- Library
- Student services and dining offerings
- Specialized aerospace and aviation labs/hangars
- Nearby access to local athletics facilities

## Shared Services Across All Campuses
- **CCSAI (Centennial College Student Association Incorporated):** student lounges, health and legal services, food bank, clubs
- **Libraries and Learning Centres:** research support, study spaces, computer access
- **Parking Services:** available at all main campuses
- **Food Services:** cafeterias and dining locations vary by campus, managed through the campus dining hub
- **Bookstores:** on-site or nearby at every campus
- **Interactive campus maps and virtual/in-person campus tours** are available for every location

## Quick Reference for the AI Receptionist
- **Business, hospitality, culinary programs →** Progress Campus
- **Health, community services programs →** Morningside Campus
- **Automotive programs →** Ashtonbee Campus
- **Media, communications, design, performing arts →** Story Arts Centre / Performing Arts Centre
- **Aviation and aerospace programs →** Downsview Campus
