# Centennial College: Academic and Administrative Departments

This document describes the academic Schools and the key administrative/student support departments at Centennial College, Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

## Academic Schools

Centennial College delivers its programs through eight academic Schools. Each School oversees a group of related diploma, advanced diploma, degree, certificate, and graduate certificate programs.

### School of Business
- Also referred to as "The Business School."
- Offers programs in accounting, marketing, human resources, international business, supply chain, and business administration.
- Includes the Honours Bachelor of Business Administration – International Business, a four-year degree.
- Prepares students for entrepreneurship and corporate careers through practical, industry-aligned coursework.

### School of Communications, Media, Arts and Design
- Focuses on storytelling, journalism, broadcasting, animation, game art, digital visual effects, and public relations.
- Home to the Honours Bachelor of Public Relations Management, offered in collaboration with the School of Business.
- Programs are primarily based at the Story Arts Centre (moving to Progress Campus in Fall 2026).

### School of Community and Health Studies
- Prepares students for careers that involve caring for and supporting others.
- Includes programs in early childhood education, social service work, child and youth care, and community development.
- Based primarily at the Morningside Campus.

### School of Engineering Technology and Applied Science
- Covers computer systems, software engineering, electronics, and applied science programs.
- Includes flagship programs such as Software Engineering Technology and Software Engineering Technology – Artificial Intelligence.
- Home to the Honours Bachelor of Information Technology (Computer and Communication Networks), a four-year degree.

### School of Hospitality, Tourism and Culinary Arts
- Trains students in culinary arts, hotel and restaurant management, and tourism.
- Uses specialized culinary labs and hospitality training facilities at Progress Campus.

### School of Transportation
- Operates the largest on-site transportation training facility in Canada.
- Covers automotive, aerospace, and motive power programs.
- Delivered across the Ashtonbee Campus (automotive) and Downsview Campus (aerospace/aviation).

### School of English and Liberal Studies
- Provides foundational academic and English-language preparation for students before they enter diploma, advanced diploma, or degree programs.
- Supports university and college preparation, general education courses, and English-language assessment.

### Part-time Learning (School of Flexible Learning)
- Delivers courses and programs for part-time and continuing-education learners.
- Offers flexible scheduling, including evening, weekend, and online course options.
- Administers Micro-Credential programs for skills-focused, short-duration learning.

## Key Administrative and Student Support Departments

### Admissions / Enrolment Services
- Handles applications, registration, fee payments, official transcripts, and graduation processing for both domestic and international applicants.
- Domestic applicants generally apply through ontariocolleges.ca; international applicants apply directly to Centennial.
- Enrolment Services is located at each of the five main campuses, with many services also available online through the myCentennial portal and CASS (Centennial Access to Student Services).

### Financial Aid / Student Financial Services
- Manages OSAP (Ontario Student Assistance Program) applications, scholarships, and bursaries.
- Assists students with tuition payment plans, fee deadlines, and financial hardship inquiries.
- Available to both domestic and eligible international students, depending on the program.

### International Education / International Student Services
- Supports international applicants through the admissions process, including document submission and study permit guidance.
- Provides settlement and immigration-related information once students arrive in Canada.
- Maintains regional International Admissions teams that correspond with specific countries and territories around the world.
- Contact: international@centennialcollege.ca

### Career Services and Co-operative Education
- Staffed by employment advisors, career counsellors, and peer career coaches.
- Offers resume and cover letter reviews, LinkedIn reviews, interview preparation, and job search planning.
- Provides access to job postings, co-op placements, and networking tools for students and graduates.
- Contact: 416-289-5233 | careerservices@centennialcollege.ca

### Other Student Support Departments
- **Centre for Accessible Learning and Counselling Services (CALCS):** provides accommodations, disability-related support, and free confidential counselling.
- **Student Experience Office (SEO):** helps resolve student-related complaints and concerns.
- **Centennial College Student Association Incorporated (CCSAI):** the student association; offers health and legal services, a food bank, and student clubs.
- **Library and Learning Centres:** provide research help, academic integrity resources, and study support across all campuses.

## Quick Reference for the AI Receptionist
- For **program-specific questions**, direct students to the relevant academic School.
- For **application, fee, or transcript questions**, direct students to Admissions / Enrolment Services.
- For **funding, OSAP, or scholarship questions**, direct students to Financial Aid / Student Financial Services.
- For **visa, study permit, or settlement questions**, direct students to the International Centre.
- For **job search or co-op questions**, direct students to Career Services.
